Add your score to today's board? It's anonymous: you get a made-up name, and nothing that identifies you is ever shown.

    Fewest guesses first, then fastest. A fresh board, and a fresh name, every morning.

    How it works

    Type a five letter word and press Enter. Blue means the right letter in the right spot, gold means the letter is in the word somewhere else, and paper means it is not in the word. A new word arrives each morning. Your guesses stay in this browser. The board only hears from you if you choose to add your score.
