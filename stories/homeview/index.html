HomeView, Apple's $299 kitchen hub, recognizes six faces and runs the lights and locks

HomeView is Apple's new small AI computer for the kitchen counter: a 7-inch screen on a speaker, with a camera and Apple's A18 chip. It runs Siri AI, recognizes up to six people by face and controls a home's lights, locks, cameras and thermostat. Apple announced it on October 13; it costs $299 and arrives November 13. [1][2]

In Apple's words

The hub: Apple HomeView is a central hub for your smart home accessories. [2] A hub lets a household control your smart home accessories while you're away from home, share control of your accessories with others, and automate your accessories to do what you want, when you want. [10]

Lights, locks, cameras, thermostat: HomeView lets users control the home by touch or with just their voice using Siri AI: viewing camera feeds, adjusting the thermostat and lights, locking doors, or closing the blinds. The Home Camera face can livestream up to nine video feeds at once. [1]

iPhone: When paired with an Apple Intelligence-enabled device, it can also draw on a user's personal context, such as their Calendar, Messages, and relevant information across their apps. [1]

Apple's cloud: Siri requests on HomeView can use on-device processing or Private Cloud Compute, which extends the privacy of the device to the cloud. [1]

Privacy

What stays in the house and what leaves it.

In the house

  • Secure ExclavesCamera and microphone feeds, in a compartment that cannot be accessed by the operating system or by Apple. [2]
  • On deviceSimple requests in the Home app: your data never leaves your device. [8]
  • Camera recordingsAnalyzed privately on your Apple devices at home. [8]

Leaves the house

  • Private Cloud ComputeUsed for complex requests. Data there is not stored nor made accessible to Apple or anyone else. [1][8]
  • Camera recordingsSent securely to iCloud through end-to-end encryption. [8]
  • Siri requestsAssociated with a random identifier, not your Apple Account. [8]

The models, on both sides: custom-built in collaboration with Google and its Gemini models, they run on device and on servers using Private Cloud Compute. [6]

More from the record

The hub line

Apple names four home hubs: HomeView, HomePod, HomePod mini and Apple TV 4K. [7] When you set up HomePod mini and HomePod, they automatically become home hubs. [10] iPad isn't supported as a home hub on the latest version of Apple Home. [11]

Chips: HomeView A18, Apple TV 4K A19, HomePod mini 2 S10. [2][3][4] HomePod mini can recognize up to six users in a household by their voice. [3] Apple TV 4K supports secure local processing of HomeKit Secure Video. [4]

Mac mini with M6 is on none of Apple's hub lists. [7][10][11] Of it, Apple says: Create your own AI agents for anything from running a household to running a business. Mac mini enables fast, private, on-device inference. [12] The new HomePod mini can serve as a speaker for Mac. [3]

The device

A 7-inch Liquid Retina screen (6.98 inches measured diagonally), 500 nits down to 0.3; the A18 chip; a 12MP Center Stage camera; four beamforming microphones; on a speaker base or wall-mounted. FaceTime, Intercom, recipes as steps with timers, and an alert to each person's iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch when it hears a smoke or carbon monoxide alarm. Setup and multiple users require a compatible iPhone or iPad. [1][2]

Security, further

Home data is stored in a way that Apple can't read it. [7] Home Collection camera footage, for users with iCloud+, is stored encrypted in iCloud for 10 days. [5]

Apple's five requirements for Private Cloud Compute, 2024: stateless computation, enforceable guarantees, no privileged runtime access, non-targetability, verifiable transparency. User devices will be willing to send data only to PCC nodes that can cryptographically attest to running publicly listed software. [14] Outside experts can continue to verify this privacy promise at any time. [6]

The models and the servers

The next generation of Apple Foundation Models will be based on Google's Gemini models and cloud technology. Google and Apple, Jan. 12, 2026. [15] In addition to its own data center infrastructure, the Company relies on third-party cloud service providers to meet these compute needs. Apple 10-Q, July 31, 2026. [16] Features on server-side models are subject to daily usage limits. Expanded access to such features will be available for a fee in the future. [1]

Who can use it

Guests can still easily interact with HomeView to control smart home accessories, check the weather, set timers, and play music, without accessing anyone's personal information. [2] Siri AI is not available for users under 13. [6] Without an Apple Intelligence iPhone or iPad, HomeView still brings Siri AI's broad world knowledge; ability to have rich, natural conversations; and improved music discovery. [1]

At the door, a chime sounds through HomeView, HomePod, or HomePod mini. [5] In the kitchen, recipes become step-by-step instructions with hands-free timer control. [1] Keep an eye on the game while you do the dishes. [2] At night, locks and motion sensors that activate when it's bedtime. [21]
