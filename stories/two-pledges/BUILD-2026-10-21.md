## What was built
`stories/two-pledges/index.html` is one self-contained page (inline CSS and JS, Google Fonts as on homeview, about 48 KB, no images to inline). It is light only (cool paper #F5F7F6, white sheets), with no brown, no dark block and no `prefers-color-scheme` block. Anthropic's lane is teal, Musk's lane is indigo, and gold is used only for hairlines and kicker dashes. Kickers use style C (Fraunces italic). Sections:
1. **Hero:** kicker, headline, lede, a who-cares line, the stamp "Not yet published" (an HTML comment marks where the push date goes), and the disclosure: "Anthropic is the company that makes Claude, the AI that helps make this site." Beside the text sit two drawn 100-square grids: 5 of 100 (plus about 20 of 100 with the earlier pledges) for Anthropic, and 1.5 of 100 (about 73 million of about 4.8 billion shares) for Musk.
2. **The record:** two columns of dated quotes in their words, each with a fold. Anthropic: LLC form, no lobbying, $200M, co-founders' 80%, the employee match, the pay line. Musk: his 4.8B shares, Bessent, the President's thank-you, Shotwell, and Sen. Moreno (the Ohio angle).
3. **The two pledges:** a drawn comparison with two lanes and four stops each (the pledge, who decides, what has to happen first, what is not known). The last leg is dashed. It runs horizontal at 1280 and vertical on phones.
4. **How it works:** two panels.
   - Anthropic: a 4-step flow from shares, through the vote gate, to the LLC, forking into nonprofits or direct distributions. Below it, a step chart: 5% today, then 7/9/11% dashed "at the board's discretion" with the baseline undisclosed.
   - Musk: a drawn Trump Account card, then a 5-step flow taken from the primary Treasury text (TD 10056). It runs from his shares to an eligible donor (government or 501(c)(3)), a Treasury acceptance agreement (class of 5,000 or more, by place and birth year), equal amounts to each account, and a hold of 5 years or until the end of the year the child turns 17. The panel is framed conditionally: "If the gift goes through Trump Accounts, as Gerstner said."
5. **Timeline (inline SVG):** a horizontal version for 761px and up, a vertical one below.
   - On the record: Sept 30 Treasury rules, Oct 18 Anthropic post, Oct 19 Musk post, Oct 21 Gerstner.
   - Still to come: stockholder vote NOT SET; Fund structure and governance "in the coming months"; Musk's gift "next year," 2027; first share to anyone NOT SET.
   - Past is drawn solid, the future dashed.
7. **Sources:** 8 numbered sources with ids `s1`..`s8`. Every inline citation is an anchor to its source (64 in-page links). A provenance and trademark line follows, then Related (who-owns-the-pipes, live) and the footer, the For AIs line and the Cloudflare beacon (pasted in directly; see the scripts section).

Motion is small. Grids fill in, cards rise and the timeline draws on. All of it is off under reduced motion or with JS off, so everything is static and visible.
