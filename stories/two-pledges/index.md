## Anthropic pledges 5% of its shares to a public benefit fund. Elon Musk pledges one SpaceX share to every kid in America.

Anthropic, the AI company, said on October 18 that 5% of its shares will go into a new fund that could give to nonprofits or make direct distributions to groups of people, once its stockholders approve. [1] The next day Elon Musk, who runs SpaceX, posted that next year he will give one of his SpaceX shares to every kid in America. [3][4]

#### A share in a Trump Account

If the gift goes through Trump Accounts, as the President posted and Gerstner said, the Treasury's rules of Sept. 30 set the path. [6][7][8]

[Chart: A drawn account card: a Trump Account for one child, holding 1 share of SpaceX stock, held for 5 years, or until the end of the year the child turns 17 if that comes first.]

1. 1 **Musk's SpaceX shares**About 4.8 billion, by an August filing. [5]
2. 2 **An eligible donor** An eligible donor is a government or nonprofit: a government, a tribal government or a 501(c)(3) organization. [7]
3. 3 **A Treasury acceptance agreement**It sets which children receive the gift: every child with an account, or those in named states or birth years (a class drawn that way needs at least 5,000 children). The stock must be publicly traded stock of a domestic corporation. [7]
4. 4 **Each child's account**The Treasury makes the contribution in an equal amount for each account beneficiary. The accounts are open to any U.S. child under 18 with a Social Security number. [6][7]
5. 5 **Held**Until the earlier of 5 years after it arrives or Dec. 31 of the year the child turns 17. [7]

The rules count a contribution as made when it is received by the Trump account, and not when the contribution is announced. [7]
