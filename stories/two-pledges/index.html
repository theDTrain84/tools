Two pledges · October 21, 2026Understanding the week, from the record

Anthropic · Elon Musk · Oct. 18 and 19, 2026

Anthropic pledges 5% of its shares to a public benefit fund. Elon Musk pledges one SpaceX share to every kid in America.

Anthropic, the AI company, said on October 18 that 5% of its shares will go into a new fund that could give to nonprofits or make direct distributions to groups of people, once its stockholders approve. [1] The next day Elon Musk, who runs SpaceX, posted that next year he will give one of his SpaceX shares to every kid in America. [3][4]

If both go through, a child's account could hold a share of SpaceX, and a fund owned by an AI company could give to charities or straight to groups of people. Neither pledge has a date for the first share to move. [1][5][6][8]

The record

In their words, October 18 to 21.

Anthropic

Post, “Investing in the public benefit,” Sunday, Oct. 18, 2026

Subject to stockholder approval, 5% of Anthropic's shares will be committed today, with an additional 2% added at the board's discretion for every 50% increase in the company's value above a set baseline. [1]
Anthropic says it might explore large donations to traditional nonprofits or direct distributions to specific populations. [1]
AI may generate enormous economic value, but it could also cause instability for many workers. [1]

Post on its Long-Term Benefit Trust, Wednesday, Oct. 21, 2026

Subject to stockholder approval, we've also committed to setting aside a portion of Anthropic's shares to Anthropic Public Benefit Fund. [2]
More from Anthropic's post

The form: the Fund is a new wholly owned entity, set up as an LLC to give adjustability between different forms of giving. [1]

Politics: None of the Anthropic equity held by the LLC will be used for lobbying or political giving, in the US or abroad. [1]

Work and jobs: Anthropic has already committed $200 million to studying and addressing this issue, but given the pace of AI progress, bolder approaches may be needed. [1]

Earlier pledges: our co-founders pledged to donate 80% of their equity holdings; employees can pledge equity which Anthropic matches. Approximately half of our employees participate in this program. Together with the Fund, roughly 20% of Anthropic shares will be dedicated to the public good. [1]

Pay: It is common practice for tech companies to offer escalating compensation awards to their executives. We are instead directing that equity to the public benefit. [1]

Elon Musk

Posts on X, Monday, Oct. 19, 2026

Next year, I will give one of my SpaceX shares to every kid in America. This will be the first of many. [3][8]
In reply to Anthropic's post (Oct. 18 or 19): I recommend giving the shares directly to the public. [4][5]

Musk did not provide further details on how his share pledge will be carried out. [4] SpaceX closed at $146.03 the Friday before, which puts one share for each of about 73 million children at about $10.7 billion. [4]

Brad Gerstner, on CNBC, Wednesday, Oct. 21, 2026

Gerstner, the Altimeter Capital chief and an architect of Trump Accounts, said the stock donations would come via Trump Accounts, and: Elon is the tip of the iceberg. [6]
More from the record

His stake: Musk currently owns about 4.8 billion SpaceX shares, according to an August SEC filing. [5]

Gerstner, Oct. 19: he suggested in a post on X that the SpaceX shares could be distributed via Trump accounts. [5]

The Treasury Secretary, Scott Bessent, on X, Oct. 19: As @TrumpAccounts continues to grow we look forward to more philanthropists joining the movement and helping children across America jumpstart their financial future. [6]

The President, on social media, Oct. 19: A BIG THANK YOU to our legendary friend, @ElonMusk, for his incredible generosity in giving SpaceX Stock to millions of American children through TRUMP ACCOUNTS! [8]

Earlier: SpaceX President and COO Gwynne Shotwell, with her husband, planned to give money to 2 million children in low-income areas, especially in Texas. [8]

In Ohio: Sen. Bernie Moreno, R-Ohio, on X, Oct. 20, called a proposal to open Trump Accounts to citizens under 40 a game-changer and said he would lead the bill in the Senate to make it happen. That change would take an act of Congress. [6]

The two pledges

What each commits, who decides, what comes first, and what is not known.

AnthropicOct. 18, 2026

  1. The pledge

    5% of Anthropic's shares to the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, an LLC the company wholly owns; 2% more for every 50% rise in the company's value above a set baseline. [1]

  2. Who decides

    Stockholders approve the 5%. The board adds each 2% at the board's discretion. Anthropic's Long-Term Benefit Trust has the authority to appoint a majority of Anthropic's board. [1][2]

  3. What has to happen first

    A stockholder vote. Anthropic also says it will share the Fund's structure and governance in the coming months. [1]

  4. What is not known

    • The date of the vote
    • The baseline, yet to be disclosed [5]
    • Who runs the Fund
    • Which nonprofits or specific populations
    • The date of the first gift

Elon MuskOct. 19, 2026

  1. The pledge

    One of his SpaceX shares to every kid in America, next year. About 73 million children; about $10.7 billion at $146.03 a share. [3][4]

  2. Who decides

    Musk. The shares are his own. Through Trump Accounts, a government or nonprofit donor and the Treasury would sign an agreement setting which children receive it. [5][7]

  3. What has to happen first

    A way to deliver it. The President posted Oct. 19 that the gift goes through TRUMP ACCOUNTS! Gerstner said Oct. 21 the donations would come via Trump Accounts. Musk gave no further details. [4][6][8]

  4. What is not known

    • How the shares move [4][5]
    • Which donor would hold them first
    • Which children count, and as of what day
    • The date, next year

How it works

A fund inside a company, and a share in a child's account.

Anthropic's Fund

Shares move into an LLC that Anthropic owns; the LLC could give. [1]

  1. 1Anthropic's sharesAnthropic is a Public Benefit Corporation, which means we're structured to balance commercial growth with our public benefit purpose. [2]
  2. 2A stockholder voteThe 5% is committed subject to stockholder approval. [1]
  3. 3The Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, an LLCRather than a 501(c)(3) or other format. No lobbying or political giving with its Anthropic equity. [1]
  4. 4Where it could go
    Large donations to traditional nonprofits
    Direct distributions to specific populations

Dashed: added at the board's discretion. The 5% and each 2% step are Anthropic's own figures; the baseline is yet to be disclosed. [1][5]

A share in a Trump Account

If the gift goes through Trump Accounts, as the President posted and Gerstner said, the Treasury's rules of Sept. 30 set the path. [6][7][8]

  1. 1Musk's SpaceX sharesAbout 4.8 billion, by an August filing. [5]
  2. 2An eligible donorAn eligible donor is a government or nonprofit: a government, a tribal government or a 501(c)(3) organization. [7]
  3. 3A Treasury acceptance agreementIt sets which children receive the gift: every child with an account, or those in named states or birth years (a class drawn that way needs at least 5,000 children). The stock must be publicly traded stock of a domestic corporation. [7]
  4. 4Each child's accountThe Treasury makes the contribution in an equal amount for each account beneficiary. The accounts are open to any U.S. child under 18 with a Social Security number. [6][7]
  5. 5HeldUntil the earlier of 5 years after it arrives or Dec. 31 of the year the child turns 17. [7]

The rules count a contribution as made when it is received by the Trump account, and not when the contribution is announced. [7]
