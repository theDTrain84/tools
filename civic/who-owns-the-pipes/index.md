**Each owner, in full**

**Oracle, 15%.** The company Larry Ellison founded in 1977. He holds 38.2 percent and is executive chair; his son David runs Skydance. TikTok's U.S. algorithm is to be secured in Oracle's U.S. cloud environment. [4] [23]

**Silver Lake, 15%.** A private investment firm that buys into technology companies, about $126 billion under management. Its chief executive, Egon Durban, sits on the venture's seven-member board. [4] [39]

**MGX, 15%.** Abu Dhabi's AI investment company, set up in March 2024 by an AI council the UAE's president created, with Mubadala and G42 as founding partners. Its chairman, Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed Al Nahyan, is Abu Dhabi's deputy ruler and the UAE's national security adviser. [45] [39]

**ByteDance, 19.9%.** The company that built TikTok, founded in 2012 by Zhang Yiming and Liang Rubo. By TikTok's count in 2024, global investors own about 60 percent of ByteDance, its co-founders 20 and its employees 20. Investors on its board include Arthur Dantchik of Susquehanna, William Ford of General Atlantic and Philippe Laffont of Coatue. Zhang Yiming is No. 24 in the world, $84 billion, by Forbes. ByteDance chooses one of the venture's seven directors. [4] [7] [45]

**The Chinese state.** Since April 2021, a company owned by three Chinese state entities holds 1 percent of Beijing ByteDance Technology, a ByteDance subsidiary in China, and the government names one of its directors, as reported by Reuters in August 2021. [45]

**Eight other investors, 35.1%** by subtraction: Dell Family Office, Vastmere Strategic Investments, Alpha Wave Partners, Revolution, Merritt Way, Via Nova, Virgo LI and NJJ Capital. The venture publishes no percentages for them. [4]

**The board and the ads**

A seven-member board; ByteDance chooses one. The White House required ByteDance to hold less than 20% of the stock as required by law. Advertising stays with TikTok global's U.S. entities, outside the venture. [4]

Among American adults who use TikTok, 55 percent regularly get news there, Pew, 2025. [26]
