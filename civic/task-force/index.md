What the Secretary announced

### A command and a study.

At Marine Corps Base Quantico, the Secretary of War, Pete Hegseth, gave a "State of the Force" address to about 600 junior officers and enlisted leaders and announced six initiatives, each with a signed memo. These two carry the three names. [1][5][6]

1 · A command

> AUTOWARCOM, the Autonomous Warfare Command: a four-star command for drones and autonomous systems, with its own manpower, budget, acquisition authorities.

RecordTarget date October 1, 2027. Needs CongressThe memo says it must secure necessary legislation. No budget, no commander named. [5][6]

**The command, in more detail**

The memo: it will define joint needs and integrate Service-provided forces in support of geographic combatant commanders. A functional command, like Special Operations or Cyber, both of which Congress created by law. A Senate committee bill already proposes one like it, voted 18 to 9 on June 11, 2026. [5][33]

Until then, the drone program office becomes Project Agincourt, run by Owen West of the Defense Innovation Unit and Navy SEAL Senior Chief Max Strasiser, reporting to the Secretary monthly. Document: "Project Agincourt - Pathway to the Autonomous Warfare Command," Sept. 30, 2026. [5][6]

Drone warfare supercharged by SI-enabled targeting is the biggest battlefield revolution in generations.

Hegseth, about 21:56 [3]

2 · A study

> Project Meridian: a study of future war, due in 120 days, January 28, 2027, as an unclassified, publicly released final report with a classified annex.

RecordDirected by Elon Musk, Palmer Luckey and Newt Gingrich; led by MITRE for the Department's technology officer. [5][6][7]

**The study, in more detail**

The memo names one person: Effective immediately, I am directing the DoW's Chief Technology Officer, Emil Michael, to commission Project Meridian: The Future of Warfare, through a partner organization. The release names the three co-directors. MITRE named itself on October 2 and listed 17 more participants. Document: "Commissioning of Project Meridian: The Future of Warfare," Sept. 30, 2026. [5][6][7]

The speech, the release and the memo agree on 120 days. The day before, the President signed an order renaming AI "Super Intelligence" across the executive branch, and six companies signed a voluntary accord at lunch, Musk for xAI. [3][31]
