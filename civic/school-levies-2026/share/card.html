From the record · Butler, Hamilton, Warren and Clermont counties · November 3, 2026

Eleven school tax questions went to voters around Cincinnati. All six earned-income taxes failed. Three of five property questions passed.

Cincinnati Public's 7 mills, Sycamore's 6.95 and Lakota's $223 million bond by 1,461 votes passed. Winton Woods' levy and Bethel-Tate's renewal failed. Unofficial until the canvass closes November 24.
