From the record · ten states · November 3, 2026

46 data-center questions were on local ballots in ten states. Of the 44 with a count, voters took the side that limits data centers in 43. Ohio had 21 of them, in 18 places, and the limiting side won all 21.

The one exception was the City of Harwood, North Dakota, 105 to 325. Two Maine towns had no count yet. Unofficial until the canvasses close.
