## What's built
- `data.json` is the record. Every figure has a value, a date, a "through" date, source ids and a VERIFIED/REPORTED tag, plus a "bought" line wherever a tracker or filing gives one.
- `src.html` is the page template: copy, CSS and a little JS.
- `build.py` renders `src.html` and `data.json` into `index.html`. It draws every chart, flow, House panel and the source list at build time as inline SVG and HTML, so the page reads the same with JS off. It numbers citations in order of first use and inlines `data.json` as `<script type="application/json" id="record">`. The build fails if any em dash, unfilled token or unknown source id is left.
- Sections, in his order:
  1. Hero: "$423 million in ads on one Ohio Senate race." with the lede and a line-art tower.
  2. Jump bar.
  3. The totals: the Senate line over 2022, 2024 and 2026, with the reserved amount as a dotted tail to an open circle, and the governor's aired and reserved bars beside it. Folds hold "how the 2026 count grew" and "two ways of counting" (AdImpact vs. FEC).
  4. The dates: Oct. 15 to Nov. 3. The hook (Oct. 20, the $4M buy, no "paid for" tag) is highlighted.
  5. Senate flows, one per side. Funder bars sit on one scale. Payer boxes are sized by Ohio dollars on one scale across the page, and ribbons run into the side total. Boxes for money with undisclosed donors are dashed and empty.
  6. Governor flow, same form. Each box carries its own report date.
  7. The House: the 8th, 7th, 1st and 10th pinned as cards. Below them, all fifteen ranked by candidate plus outside money (stacked bars), then a 15-button picker.
