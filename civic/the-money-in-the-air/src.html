Ohio, the 2026 election

At least {{total}} spent on Ohio's 2026 races for Senate, governor and the U.S. House.

That is what the candidates' campaigns and the outside groups backing or attacking them have reported spending, to the Federal Election Commission and the Ohio Secretary of State, before Election Day on November 3. The Senate race accounts for {{total.races.0}} of it, the race for governor {{total.races.1}}, and the fifteen U.S. House races {{total.races.2}}.

The total, race by race

By the filings, the Senate race has drawn 3.8 times the money spent on the race for governor. Outside groups make most of the difference: $352.9 million in the Senate race, $44.6 million in the governor's race. {{total.why}}

Two ways of counting the same race

AdImpact estimates the value of the airtime and digital space bought, including reservations. The Federal Election Commission counts what each committee reports it spent. Through October 23, 2026, {{senate.fec_outside.filers|n}} outside committees had reported {{senate.fec_outside}} in the Senate general election to the FEC, and the two campaigns had spent $79.6 million through October 14. [[c:votelog,fec-brown,fec-husted]] Ads from 501(c)(4) nonprofits that do not expressly call for a vote are reported to the FEC only in part, or not at all. Both counts are correct for what they count.

The House

Ohio's 15 House races: $16.8 million from outside groups in the 7th, $10,178 in the 8th.
