## Method notes
- **Senate outside money:** Votelog's Oct. 23 compilation, the same source and scope as before. I cross-checked it against the FEC's 24- and 48-hour bulk file: SLF, WinSenate, Defend American Jobs, No Going Back, Duty and Country, 75 PAC, America PAC, Ohio Flyer, Defend the Vote, Digital Freedom Fund, LCV, Sentinel and AB PAC all match to the dollar. AFP Action and Lower Costs Now run higher in the raw file, probably because amendments are not netted there, so the page uses Votelog's figures. "All other committees" recomputes: R $11.4M, D $18.1M.
- **House outside money:** the Oct. 21 desk sums, plus every 24- and 48-hour row received Oct. 21 and 22 in the FEC bulk file (updated Oct. 23), with two exceptions:
  - Rolling Sea Action Fund's A3 amendment (OH-13) only restates the $115,000 already counted, so it adds nothing.
  - Senate Conservatives Fund (OH-04) re-filed all its Jordan rows. Only the rows dated Sept. 1 and later are new ($976.76), because the desk already had July and August ($620.63).
  - Pre-general regular reports (Oct. 1 to 14) are not yet processed. The desk rule, where a regular report replaces 24- and 48-hour notices, has not been re-run for that period.
- **House candidates:** FEC candidate summary file (webl26, updated Oct. 23) for every committee the FEC has processed. Landsman, Conroy, Merrin and Jukic were not yet processed, so each is the Sept. 30 summary plus the Oct. 1 to 14 receipts from their raw pre-general e-filings. That method gives the same result as the FEC's for Davidson: $751,312.88 + $5,853.42 = $757,166.30. house.note says this.
- **Funders where the FEC has not processed October reports:** the 2026 aggregate the committee itself reported in its raw e-filing, labeled "as e-filed" on the page. Two figures are additions of two records, and each says so on the page:
  - MAGA Inc.'s donors: Aug. 31 processed totals plus Sept. 1 to Oct. 14 rows.
  - LCV Inc.: Votelog's $18,020,601 through Sept. 30 plus $8M on the pre-general.
