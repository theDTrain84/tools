## How the total is built
**The defined count (on the page, word for word in `data.json` `total.count`):** money spent on Ohio's 2026 races for U.S. Senate, governor and U.S. House, as the spenders reported it. That covers the campaigns of the candidates on the November 3 ballot and the outside groups that reported spending for or against them, over this election cycle, with the May primary included.

- For outside groups, only what each group reported spending on these races counts. Money one group gave another is never counted, so MAGA Inc.'s $200 million to No Going Back PAC is not counted twice.
- A campaign's spending is everything its committee paid out.

7. **OH-01, new and not added:** Spectrum (Oct. 19) reports that CLF "canceled all remaining advertising" in the 1st after announcing at least $4M. Recommend: add one line to the OH-01 panel if you want it.
