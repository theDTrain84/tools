The memo, October 12

### Reduce duplicative work.

Kroger announced the cuts to employees on an all-company call on Monday, October 12, and in a memo from Foran the same day. [1][2]

From: Greg Foran, CEO · To: Kroger staff · Oct. 12, 2026 · excerpt

Simplifying our operations is critical. Simplifying means making faster decisions and deploying more resources where they matter most, so that we deliver lower prices and a better experience for our customers. Our company has more layers, more handoffs and more overhead than we need, so we are addressing this issue.

Technology is reshaping what's possible. The way work gets done is changing across every industry; we must adapt and take advantage of technology. We need to adopt capabilities, reduce duplicative work and look closely at where work is best fit to be done.

Kroger, via Business Insider, Oct. 12, 2026 [1]

The memo text Kroger shared says technology. Reuters wrote that the cuts come as the grocer integrates AI into its workflow; Bloomberg News wrote that the memo's point about technology includes the rise of artificial intelligence. Kroger's own plan for AI is its September 22 release. [1][4][5][11]
