Kroger's 3,000 · October 16, 2026Understanding the week, from the record

Cincinnati · Oct. 12 to 14, 2026

3,000 of Kroger's 8,200 corporate jobs will be cut. Over three years, citing technology.

Kroger, the company behind 2,697 supermarkets from Cincinnati to Los Angeles, told staff on October 12 that about 3,000 of its 8,200 corporate jobs will be cut over three years. Its chief executive, Greg Foran, wrote that technology is changing how work gets done. Three weeks earlier the company laid out its plan for AI in its app and its stores.

The memo, October 12

Reduce duplicative work.

Kroger announced the cuts to employees on an all-company call on Monday, October 12, and in a memo from Foran the same day. [1][2]

From: Greg Foran, CEO · To: Kroger staff · Oct. 12, 2026 · excerpt

Simplifying our operations is critical. Simplifying means making faster decisions and deploying more resources where they matter most, so that we deliver lower prices and a better experience for our customers. Our company has more layers, more handoffs and more overhead than we need, so we are addressing this issue.

Technology is reshaping what's possible. The way work gets done is changing across every industry; we must adapt and take advantage of technology. We need to adopt capabilities, reduce duplicative work and look closely at where work is best fit to be done.

Kroger, via Business Insider, Oct. 12, 2026 [1]

The memo text Kroger shared says technology. Reuters wrote that the cuts come as the grocer integrates AI into its workflow; Bloomberg News wrote that the memo's point about technology includes the rise of artificial intelligence. Kroger's own plan for AI is its September 22 release. [1][4][5][11]

In the second quarter of 2025, Kroger cut its corporate administrative team by nearly 1,000 associates, at a cost of $47 million in severance, per its annual report; the memo went out on August 26, 2025. About 200 of those jobs were in Cincinnati, per WCPO. [12][14][15]

3The plan

What Kroger says AI will do, and what it says about the jobs.

Kroger set out its AI strategy at the GroceryShop conference in Las Vegas on September 22, 2026. [11]

The AI plan · in the app and the store

Kroger's AI Shopping Assistant is helping customers plan meals for the week, find new recipes, and build a cart around budget and dietary needs as well as preferences.Kroger release, Sept. 22, 2026 [11]

The company is also working on improving agents for frontline teams to cut through the noise of dozens of daily notifications and tasks and surface only the most important information.Same release [11]

AI literacy training and tools for more than 400,000 associates across the enterprise.Kroger release, Sept. 22, 2026 [11]

The release does not mention corporate jobs, checkout or the pharmacy. [11]

Chief digital officer, The Kroger Co. Yael Cosset

Executive vice president and chief digital officer. He described the plan at GroceryShop in Las Vegas: a shopping assistant in the Kroger app, agents for store teams, and AI training and tools for more than 400,000 associates. [11][12]

At our core, the human connection to a customer or fellow associate will never be replaced by AI. Instead, AI will give our associates more time and insight to create that connection.

Yael Cosset · Kroger release, Sept. 22, 2026 [11]

More from Kroger's releases and its annual report

Conversational search helps customers plan meals, discover relevant products and find what they need more easily. Kroger release, Sept. 22, 2026 [11]

Kroger will use Google software to analyze interactions and intent on calls made by customers to stores. Kroger release with Google Cloud, Jan. 11, 2026 [22]

Grocery as a managed service: Rather than formulating a weekly shopping list, customers may choose to share their goals and preferences, allowing AI agents to assist with planning and completing their shopping. [11]

Kroger's annual report lists AI shopping agents among its risks: If customers increasingly delegate purchasing decisions to AI agents that prioritize price, speed or other factors over retailer preference or brand loyalty, we could become disintermediated from the customer relationship. [12]

4At the register

What changes, from the record.

If you shop at Kroger

Kroger says its AI shopping assistant plans meals and builds a cart around a budget, and since June 25, 2026, Points come off the grocery bill at $1 per 100, up to $10 a day. On September 11, Foran told analysts that savings from reductions in head count go to shelf prices. [9][11][23]

If you work in a Kroger store

The 3,000 are corporate roles, and the memo says the savings let Kroger continue to invest in our frontline associates. The AI plan offers AI training and tools to more than 400,000 associates and describes agents to cut dozens of daily notifications and tasks for store teams. [2][3][11]

If you live in Cincinnati

About 4,800 of Kroger's corporate and office jobs were in Greater Cincinnati before October 12, per the Courier as relayed by Hoodline. How many of the 3,000 fall there has not been said; in 2025, about 200 of nearly 1,000 corporate cuts did, per WCPO. [13][15]

If you fill prescriptions at Kroger

Since August 24, 2026, groceries and eligible prescriptions can arrive in one Instacart delivery from more than 2,200 Kroger pharmacies, filled and verified by Kroger pharmacy teams. The September AI plan does not mention the pharmacy. [11][24]
